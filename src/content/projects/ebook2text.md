## Overview

**Ebook2Text** is a focused Python utility designed to convert diverse digital publication formats (EPUB, DOCX, PDF, and plain TXT) into a clean, normalized plaintext and Markdown format suitable for indexing, search, and LLM fine-tuning.

### Technical Highlights

- **Multi-Format Ingestion**: Robust parsers for EPUB packages, Word OpenXML documents, and PDF text streams.
- **Vision-Assisted OCR**: Integrated vision-capable AI OCR to extract high-accuracy text from scanned documents or embedded image diagrams.
- **Text Normalization**: Automatic detection and standardization of curly quotes, em-dashes, and inconsistent typography.
- **CLI Ergonomics**: Packaged as a lightweight command-line tool with zero unnecessary bloat.
